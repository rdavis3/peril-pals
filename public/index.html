THE ENTIRE RULEBOOK

Look. Remember. Move.

  1. 1
    LOOK while dangers are visible

    Solo starts with untimed practice. Later previews shrink from 8 to 5 seconds.

  2. 2
    REMEMBER when they disappear

    Use two memory markers, or ping private dangers to teammates.

  3. 3
    MOVE BIMO one adjacent tile

    The danger pattern changes after every move, so keep looking.

  4. 4
    Collect three cores, then escape

    Three mistakes drain BIMO’s signal. Every map has a safe route.
